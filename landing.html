Questions, answered

Is Finora free?

Yes. Every feature is free for now, and we do not take any payment.

Does Jarvis change my data on its own?

No. Jarvis shows a preview and saves only after you confirm. It cannot delete transactions or change your password.

Why do I need to verify my email?

It keeps your account yours and lets you reset your password if you forget it.

Does Finora move my money?

No. Finora records and analyses your spending. It never connects to your bank or makes payments.
